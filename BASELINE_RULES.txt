SHOW REPURPOSER PROTOTYPE R1 — APPROVED VIDEO BASELINE RULES

- V11 full-frame protected source-video presentation.
- Upper black zone reserved for dynamic hit aids.
- HIT = confidently identified card with current TCGplayer NM market value >= $1.00.
- Aid starts when card is fully revealed.
- Aid duration ~3.6 seconds, then clears.
- Price requires confident card + set + printing/variant + foil/nonfoil + NM match.
- Foil and non-foil are separate product records. Never default an unknown finish to non-foil.
- When the exact finish-specific market price cannot be verified, identify the finish and omit the value.
- No hit aid may appear before the qualifying card is fully revealed.
- Small circular customer logo badge covers lower-right source-app diagonal-arrow/control area.
- Lower visual aid remains present and is customer-editable; it may be hidden when desired.
- Main footage must remain unobstructed.
- No generic center overlays.
- Export retains the source clip's audio when supported by the browser.

Prototype R1 note: automatic candidate recognition/current-price retrieval requires the next live data/recognition layer. R1 implements the approved rendering, timeline review, finish-specific verification gates, project saving, audio-preserving export, and confirmation controls without fabricating recognition or pricing.
